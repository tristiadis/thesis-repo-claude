# For Students

## Getting Started (Students)

The Thesis Repository System allows you to submit your thesis electronically and track its review status. Your thesis will be made publicly available once approved by administrators.

### System Requirements

- **Web Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest version)
- **Internet Connection**: Stable connection required for file uploads
- **PDF Files**: Your thesis files must be in PDF format
- **File Size**: Maximum 10MB per file

### Account Requirements

You need an active student account to submit theses. Contact your department administrator if you don't have an account.

## Creating an Account

**Note**: Student accounts are typically created by administrators. If registration is enabled:

1. Navigate to the homepage
2. Click **"Register"** or **"Sign Up"** button
3. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Username**: Choose a unique username (3-30 characters, alphanumeric)
   - **Email**: Your institutional email address
   - **Full Name**: Your complete name as it appears on official documents
   - **Student ID (NIM)**: Your official student identification number
   - **Password**: Choose a strong password (minimum 8 characters, must include uppercase, lowercase, and number)
   - **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password
4. Click **"Register"** button
5. You should receive a confirmation message
6. Check your email for verification (if email verification is enabled)

### Password Requirements

Your password must meet these criteria:
- Minimum 8 characters long
- At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
- At least one lowercase letter (a-z)
- At least one number (0-9)
- Special characters recommended but not required

**Example**: `MyPassword123`

## Logging In

### First Time Login

1. Navigate to the system homepage
2. Click **"Login"** button in the top navigation
3. Enter your credentials:
   - **Username**: Your assigned or chosen username
   - **Password**: Your password
4. Click **"Login"** button
5. If successful, you'll be redirected to your student dashboard

### Changing Your Password

For security, change your password after first login:

1. Click your username in the top-right corner
2. Select **"Profile"** or **"Settings"**
3. Click **"Change Password"**
4. Enter:
   - **Current Password**
   - **New Password**
   - **Confirm New Password**
5. Click **"Update Password"**

### Forgot Password

If you've forgotten your password:

1. Click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Click **"Send Reset Link"**
4. Check your email for password reset instructions
5. Click the link in the email (valid for 1 hour)
6. Enter your new password
7. Click **"Reset Password"**

## Submitting a Thesis

### Before You Submit

Prepare the following information and files:

#### Required Information:
- **Thesis Title** (Indonesian) - At least 10 characters
- **Thesis Title** (English) - Optional but recommended
- **Abstract** (Indonesian) - Minimum 100 characters
- **Abstract** (English) - Optional but recommended
- **Keywords** - 3-5 keywords, comma-separated
- **Graduation Year**
- **Defense Date** - Date of your thesis defense
- **Advisor(s)** - At least one advisor required
- **Examiners** - At least one examiner required
- **Faculty and Department**

#### Required Files:
- PDF files for your thesis chapters
- File size: Maximum 10MB per file
- File types supported: PDF only

### Step-by-Step Submission Process

#### Step 1: Access the Submission Form

1. Login to your account
2. From your dashboard, click **"Submit New Thesis"** button
3. Or navigate to **Student > Submit Thesis** from the menu

#### Step 2: Enter Basic Information

Fill in the thesis details:

1. **Faculty**: Select your faculty from dropdown
   - The list will show all active faculties

2. **Department**: Select your department
   - This dropdown updates based on selected faculty
   - Only shows departments within your faculty

3. **Thesis Title (Indonesian)**: Enter your thesis title
   - Minimum 10 characters
   - Maximum 500 characters
   - Use proper capitalization
   - Example: "Sistem Manajemen Repositori Skripsi Berbasis Web"

4. **Thesis Title (English)**: (Optional)
   - Translation of your Indonesian title
   - Example: "Web-Based Thesis Repository Management System"

5. **Abstract (Indonesian)**: Enter your thesis abstract
   - Minimum 100 characters
   - Maximum 5000 characters
   - Briefly describe your research objectives, methods, and findings

6. **Abstract (English)**: (Optional)
   - English translation of your abstract

7. **Keywords (Indonesian)**: Enter 3-5 keywords
   - Comma-separated
   - Example: "repositori, skripsi, manajemen, web"

8. **Keywords (English)**: (Optional)
   - Example: "repository, thesis, management, web"

#### Step 3: Enter Academic Information

1. **Graduation Year**: Select your graduation year
   - Choose from dropdown (range: 2000 - current year + 1)

2. **Defense Date**: Click calendar icon and select date
   - Format: DD/MM/YYYY
   - Must be a past or current date

3. **Primary Advisor**: Select from dropdown
   - Shows lecturers from your department
   - Required field
   - Format shown: "Name (NIDN)"

4. **Secondary Advisor**: (Optional)
   - Select if you have a co-advisor

5. **Primary Examiner**: Select first examiner
   - Required field

6. **Second Examiner**: (Optional)
   - Select if applicable

7. **Third Examiner**: (Optional)
   - Select if applicable

#### Step 4: Upload Thesis Files

You can upload multiple PDF files for different chapters:

1. **File Type**: Select file type from dropdown:
   - Cover (Halaman Sampul)
   - Chapter 1 (Bab 1 - Pendahuluan)
   - Chapter 2 (Bab 2 - Tinjauan Pustaka)
   - Chapter 3 (Bab 3 - Metodologi)
   - Chapter 4 (Bab 4 - Hasil dan Pembahasan)
   - Chapter 5 (Bab 5 - Kesimpulan)
   - Bibliography (Daftar Pustaka)
   - Appendix (Lampiran)
   - Other (Lainnya)

2. **Choose File**: Click **"Choose File"** button
   - Browse and select your PDF file
   - Maximum size: 10MB
   - Only PDF files accepted

3. **Access Level**: Choose access level for this file:
   - **PUBLIC**: Accessible to everyone immediately
   - **EMBARGOED**: Restricted until specified date
   - **RESTRICTED**: Admin-only access

4. **Embargo Until**: (If EMBARGOED selected)
   - Select date when file becomes public
   - Must be a future date

5. **Embargo Reason**: (If EMBARGOED selected)
   - Explain why the file is embargoed
   - Example: "Contains preliminary research data pending publication"

6. **Add File**: Click **"Add File"** to add to upload queue

7. **Repeat**: Click **"Add Another File"** to upload more chapters

#### Step 5: Review Your Submission

Before submitting:

1. Review all information for accuracy
2. Check that all required fields are filled
3. Verify file uploads are complete
4. Ensure correct advisors and examiners are selected

#### Step 6: Submit for Review

1. If you want to save as draft:
   - Click **"Save as Draft"** button
   - You can edit later before submitting

2. To submit for review:
   - Click **"Submit for Review"** button
   - Confirm submission in popup dialog
   - Status changes to "PENDING"

### After Submission

Once submitted:

1. You'll see a confirmation message
2. Status changes to **"Pending Review"**
3. You cannot edit the submission
4. Wait for administrator review
5. You'll receive notifications about status changes

## Managing Your Submissions

### Viewing Your Submissions

1. Login to your account
2. Navigate to **"My Dashboard"** or **"My Submissions"**
3. You'll see a list of all your thesis submissions

### Understanding Status Indicators

Each submission has a status:

| Status | Icon | Description | Actions Available |
|--------|------|-------------|-------------------|
| **DRAFT** | 📝 Draft | Not yet submitted | Edit, Submit, Delete |
| **PENDING** | ⏳ Pending | Under review | View only |
| **APPROVED** | ✅ Approved | Published publicly | View, Download |
| **REJECTED** | ❌ Rejected | Needs revision | View, Edit, Resubmit |

### Editing a Draft

If status is **DRAFT**:

1. Click **"Edit"** button on the submission
2. Make your changes
3. Click **"Save as Draft"** or **"Submit for Review"**

### Editing a Rejected Submission

If status is **REJECTED**:

1. Click **"View Details"** to see rejection reason
2. Read administrator's notes carefully
3. Click **"Edit and Resubmit"**
4. Make required changes
5. Click **"Resubmit for Review"**

### Deleting a Draft

⚠️ **Warning**: This action cannot be undone!

1. Click **"Delete"** button on draft submission
2. Confirm deletion in popup
3. Submission is permanently removed

**Note**: You can only delete DRAFT submissions. Submitted, approved, or rejected theses cannot be deleted by students.

## Student FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Do I need an account to browse theses?**
A: No, browsing and searching are public. You only need an account to submit your own thesis.

**Q: Can I submit multiple theses?**
A: Yes, if you have multiple thesis projects (e.g., undergraduate and graduate).

**Q: What file formats are accepted?**
A: Only PDF files are currently accepted.

**Q: What's the maximum file size?**
A: Each file can be up to 10MB. If your file is larger, try compressing it or splitting into chapters.

**Q: Can I update my thesis after it's approved?**
A: No. Once approved, the thesis is published and cannot be modified. Submit carefully!

### Submission Questions

**Q: I accidentally submitted the wrong file. What do I do?**
A: Contact an administrator immediately. They may be able to reject the submission so you can correct it.

**Q: Can I submit my thesis in English?**
A: Yes. Fill in the English title and abstract fields. The Indonesian fields are required but can be translations.

**Q: What if my advisor/examiner is not in the list?**
A: Contact your department administrator to add the lecturer to the system first.

**Q: How long does review take?**
A: Review time varies. Contact your department if there's no update after 2 weeks.

**Q: Can I delete my submission after it's approved?**
A: No. Approved theses are part of the permanent academic record.

### Technical Questions

**Q: The upload keeps failing. What should I do?**
A: Try these solutions:
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure file is PDF format
- Ensure file is under 10MB
- Try a different browser
- Clear browser cache
- Disable browser extensions

**Q: I can't see the submit button. Why?**
A: Make sure you've:
- Filled all required fields (marked with *)
- Selected faculty and department
- Added at least one file
- Entered valid graduation year and defense date

**Q: Can I use mobile to submit?**
A: Yes, the system is mobile-responsive. However, desktop is recommended for file uploads.

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login page and follow the email instructions.

### File Access Questions

**Q: What is an embargo?**
A: An embargo temporarily restricts access to a file until a specified date. Use this if your research contains unpublished data.

**Q: When should I use RESTRICTED access?**
A: Use RESTRICTED for files containing sensitive data that should never be public. Only administrators can access.

**Q: Can I change file access level after submission?**
A: No. Only administrators can modify access levels after submission.

**Q: What happens when an embargo expires?**
A: The file automatically becomes PUBLIC on the expiration date.

## Reviewing Thesis Submissions

### Accessing Pending Reviews

1. Dashboard shows count of pending reviews
2. Click **"Review Pending Theses"** button
3. Or go to **Admin > Theses > Pending Reviews**

### Review Queue

The pending review page shows:

- **Submission Date**: When submitted
- **Student Name**: Who submitted
- **Thesis Title**: Title of thesis
- **Department**: Student's department
- **Days Waiting**: How long in queue
- **Actions**: Preview, Review

- Date (oldest first - recommended)

- Department

### Reviewing a Submission

#### Step 1: Open Thesis Details

1. Click **"Review"** button on submission
2. Review page opens with all details

#### Step 2: Verify Information

Check the following:

**Metadata Accuracy**:
- [ ] Title is appropriate and correctly formatted
- [ ] Abstract is complete and coherent
- [ ] Keywords are relevant
- [ ] Graduation year is correct
- [ ] Defense date is valid

**Academic Personnel**:
- [ ] Advisors are correct
- [ ] Examiners are appropriate
- [ ] All selections match official records

**File Quality**:
- [ ] All required files uploaded
- [ ] Files are readable PDFs
- [ ] No corrupted files
- [ ] File content matches declared file type

#### Step 3: Preview Files

1. Click **"Preview"** on each file
2. PDF opens in browser
3. Check:
   - File is not corrupted
   - Content is appropriate
   - No blank pages
   - Proper formatting

#### Step 4: Make Decision

##### To Approve:

1. Click **"Approve"** button
2. Optional: Add approval notes
3. Click **"Confirm Approval"**
4. Thesis is published immediately
5. Student receives approval notification

##### To Reject:

1. Click **"Reject"** button
2. **Required**: Enter rejection reason
   - Be specific and constructive
   - List what needs correction
   - Provide guidance
3. Example rejection reasons:
   - "Abstract is too short. Please expand to at least 200 words."
   - "Chapter 3 file is corrupted. Please re-upload."
   - "Incorrect advisor selected. Should be Prof. Smith, not Dr. Jones."
4. Click **"Confirm Rejection"**
5. Student can edit and resubmit

#### Step 5: Post-Review Actions

After approval:
- Thesis appears in public repository
- Downloads and views are tracked
- Statistics are recorded

After rejection:
- Student sees rejection reason
- Student can edit and resubmit
- Resubmission goes back to review queue

### Batch Review Actions

For multiple submissions:

1. Select checkboxes on multiple theses
2. Choose bulk action:
   - **Approve Selected**: Approve all at once
   - **Export Selected**: Export metadata
3. Confirm action

**Note**: Bulk rejection not available - each rejection requires individual reasoning.

## Setting Embargo and Access Control

### Understanding Access Levels

**PUBLIC**:
- Accessible to anyone
- No restrictions
- Appears in search results
- Available for download

**EMBARGOED**:
- Restricted until specified date
- Only accessible by:
  - Administrators (always)
  - Public users (after expiration)
- Use cases:
  - Pending patent applications
  - Data awaiting publication
  - Institutional requirements

**RESTRICTED**:
- Permanently restricted
- Only accessible by administrators
- Does not appear in public search
- Use cases:
  - Sensitive research data
  - Classified information
  - Privacy concerns

### Setting Embargo on a File

#### During Review:

1. Open thesis for review
2. Find file in files list
3. Click **"Edit Access"** on file
4. Select **"EMBARGOED"**
5. Set **"Embargo Until"** date (calendar picker)
6. Enter **"Embargo Reason"**:
   - Example: "Research data pending journal publication (expected March 2026)"
7. Click **"Update Access"**

#### After Approval:

1. Go to **Admin > Theses > All Theses**
2. Find and click thesis
3. Click **"Files"** tab
4. Click **"Edit Access"** on specific file
5. Modify access level and embargo date
6. Click **"Update"**

### Lifting Embargo Early

If embargo needs to be lifted before expiration:

1. Find thesis and file
2. Click **"Edit Access"**
3. Change to **"PUBLIC"**
4. Remove embargo date
5. Optional: Add note explaining early lift
6. Click **"Update"**
7. File becomes immediately public

### Managing Restricted Files

For permanently restricted files:

1. Edit file access
2. Select **"RESTRICTED"**
3. Clear embargo date (not applicable)
4. Enter restriction reason
5. Click **"Update"**

**Admin Access**: Administrators can always download restricted files by:
1. Viewing thesis details as admin
2. Clicking **"Download (Admin)"** button

### Embargo Expiration Notifications

System automatically:
- Checks for expiring embargoes daily
- Sends admin notification 7 days before expiration
- Automatically makes file PUBLIC on expiration date
- Logs all access level changes

View upcoming expirations:
1. Go to **Admin > Reports > Embargo Report**
2. See list of embargoes expiring in next 30 days

## Admin FAQ

### User Management

**Q: Should I delete inactive student accounts?**
A: No. Deactivate instead to preserve thesis submission records.

**Q: Can students have the same email?**
A: No. Each email must be unique across all users.

**Q: How do I promote a user to admin?**
A: This requires database access. Contact system administrator or modify via Prisma Studio.

**Q: Can students see other students' drafts?**
A: No. Students only see their own drafts. Only approved theses are public.

### Review Questions

**Q: What if I approve a thesis by mistake?**
A: Contact system administrator to revert status. No built-in "unapprove" function to maintain integrity.

**Q: How long should review take?**
A: Best practice: within 3-5 business days. Long delays frustrate students.

**Q: Can I assign reviews to other admins?**
A: Not currently. Any admin can review any submission (first-come-first-served).

**Q: What if student uploads wrong file?**
A: Reject with clear instructions. Student can resubmit corrected version.

### Access Control

**Q: Can I change embargo date after setting it?**
A: Yes. Edit file access anytime to extend or shorten embargo.

**Q: What happens if I set embargo date in the past?**
A: System treats it as expired immediately. File becomes PUBLIC.

**Q: Can students see restricted files?**
A: No. Only administrators can access RESTRICTED files.

**Q: How do I handle copyright issues?**
A: Set file to RESTRICTED pending resolution. Add note in file description.

### Statistics & Reports

**Q: Are IP addresses logged?**
A: Yes, but hashed for privacy. Raw IPs are never stored.

**Q: Can I see who downloaded a specific file?**
A: Only anonymized data (hashed IP, date, time). No personal identification.

**Q: How far back do statistics go?**
A: Since system installation. Historical data is never deleted.

**Q: Can I export raw database data?**
A: Yes, use Prisma Studio or database tools. Requires technical knowledge.

**Q: System is slow. What can I do?**

1. Check number of active users

## Exporting Citations

### Available Citation Formats

Export thesis metadata in standard citation formats:

1. **BibTeX** (.bib)
   - For LaTeX documents
   - Popular in computer science

2. **RIS** (.ris)
   - For EndNote, Mendeley, Zotero
   - Universal format

3. **EndNote XML** (.xml)
   - Native EndNote format
   - Most features

4. **JSON** (.json)
   - For developers and custom tools
   - Machine-readable

### How to Export

1. View thesis details page
2. Scroll to **"Export Citation"** section
3. Click format button (BibTeX, RIS, EndNote, or JSON)
4. File downloads automatically
5. Import into your reference manager

### Using Exported Citations

#### BibTeX (LaTeX):

1. Download .bib file
2. Place in same folder as your LaTeX document
3. Reference in LaTeX:
   ```latex
   \bibliography{thesis-citation}
   \cite{AuthorYear}
   ```

#### RIS (EndNote/Mendeley/Zotero):

1. Download .ris file
2. Open your reference manager
3. File > Import > Select .ris file
4. Citation appears in library

#### EndNote XML:

1. Download .xml file
2. Open EndNote
3. File > Import > File
4. Select downloaded XML
5. Import directly to your library

#### JSON (Custom):

1. Download .json file
2. Parse with your application
3. Extract needed fields

### Citation Content

Exported citation includes:

- Thesis title
- Author name
- Graduation year
- University name
- Department
- Thesis type (Undergraduate/Graduate)
- Advisors
- Abstract
- Keywords
- URL to thesis page

### Citation Statistics

Your citation export is tracked (anonymously) to help:
- Measure research impact
- Identify popular theses
- Understand user behavior

No personal data is collected.
